About

I have spent more than 15 years designing, building and running software in Brazil and Sweden. Most of it has been backend work on the JVM: APIs, services that talk to each other over Kafka, and the pipelines and clusters that ship them.

My work covers the full lifecycle, from design to production operations. I also apply the same discipline to AI-assisted development: agentic workflows, LLM-based developer tooling, and coding agents that work from clear tasks, automated tests and verification steps.
